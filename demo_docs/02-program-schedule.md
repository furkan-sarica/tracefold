## Room notes

Aurora Auditorium and Studios N1–N4 are on the ground floor of Orion Building. Nova Dining Hall is across the covered courtyard. The Fabrication Studio is on level B1 and is access-controlled; participants need both a badge and a completed safety sign-off.

## Schedule changes

The Program Office may move a session for safety or operational reasons. Confirmed changes appear on the lobby display and are sent by email. If the two disagree, contact the Program Office using the Contacts Directory. Mentors cannot authorize late arrival, room changes, or deadline extensions.
