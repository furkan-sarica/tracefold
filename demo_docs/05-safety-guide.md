# Northstar Summer Lab 2026 — Safety and Emergency Guide

> This document describes procedures at a fictional campus. It is demo content, not real emergency guidance.

## Immediate emergencies

For fire, smoke, a serious injury, or an immediate threat:

1. Move away from danger without collecting personal belongings.
2. Activate the nearest alarm if it has not sounded.
3. Call the fictional campus emergency extension **5555** from a campus phone, or **+90 312 555 0155** from a mobile.
4. Follow staff instructions and go to the signed assembly point.

The Orion Building assembly point is **North Lawn beside the blue beacon**. Cedar House residents assemble at **East Courtyard beside the bicycle shelter**. Do not re-enter a building until the Safety Officer gives permission.

## Medical support

The Campus Health Room is on the ground floor of Nova Hall and is open weekdays from 08:30 to 17:30. For non-urgent support, contact the Campus Nurse using the Contacts Directory. Participants should carry prescribed medication themselves; staff do not store or dispense personal prescriptions.

All injuries, including minor workshop cuts, must be reported to the supervising instructor before leaving the room. Participants may request a private wellbeing conversation through Participant Support.

## Mandatory induction and workshop access

The safety induction takes place at **09:00 on Wednesday, 5 August** in Aurora Auditorium. Completion is required before entering the Fabrication Studio. Anyone who misses it must arrange a make-up induction with the Safety Officer and continue software-only work until signed off.

In the Fabrication Studio:

- Wear closed-toe shoes and the supplied safety glasses.
- Tie back long hair and secure loose clothing.
- Use a machine only after a staff demonstration and explicit permission.
- Never work alone or bypass guards, interlocks, or extraction systems.
- Stop work immediately if equipment behaves unexpectedly.

Personal soldering irons, batteries under repair, chemicals, laser devices, and drones are not permitted. The Equipment Guide lists what the program supplies.

## Fire alarm and evacuation

Treat every alarm as real. Use stairs, not lifts. Participants who need evacuation assistance should wait at the signed refuge point and use the call panel; an assigned marshal will assist them. Staff run a scheduled evacuation drill at **11:30 on Tuesday, 4 August**. The drill does not cancel morning sessions, and attendance is recorded at the assembly point.

## Severe weather and service interruption

If campus operations change, the Program Office sends a verified email and updates the Orion lobby display. This corpus does not contain a weather forecast, temperature prediction, or live campus status. Participants should not rely on a generated answer for real-time conditions.

## Reporting hazards

Report blocked exits, spills, damaged cables, missing guards, or unsafe behavior to the nearest staff member and the Safety Officer. A conduct concern may also be reported through the confidential routes in the Code of Conduct. In an immediate threat, the emergency procedure takes priority over confidential reporting: call **5555** from a campus phone or **+90 312 555 0155** from a mobile before making a conduct report.
