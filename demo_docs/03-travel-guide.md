# Northstar Summer Lab 2026 — Travel Guide

> This is synthetic travel information for a fictional program. Do not use it for real journeys.

## Arrival day: Sunday, 2 August

The complimentary Northstar arrival shuttle departs **Northstar Central Station, Bay 4**, at **13:30, 16:30, and 19:30** on Sunday, 2 August. The ride to Meridian Innovation Campus normally takes 25 minutes. Participants should arrive at Bay 4 at least 10 minutes before departure and show their program confirmation to the driver.

Each participant may bring one suitcase and one carry-on item. Bicycles, oversized sports equipment, and unboxed technical equipment cannot travel on the shuttle. The shuttle is wheelchair accessible when requested by 24 July through Accessibility Support in the Contacts Directory.

There is **no Northstar-operated airport shuttle**. From the fictional Meridian Airport, participants should use the public A2 coach to Northstar Central Station and transfer to Bay 4. Tracefold should not infer coach fares or live departure times because those details are not included in this corpus.

## Late arrivals

The last scheduled arrival shuttle leaves at 19:30. A participant who expects to reach campus after the Cedar House check-in desk closes must do both of the following:

1. Call the Residence Duty Phone at **+90 312 555 0149** before **19:30** to arrange key collection.
2. Send the revised arrival time to the Program Office at **program@northstar.example**.

The relevant phone number and email address are in the Contacts Directory. Taxi or public-transport costs caused by a late arrival are the participant's responsibility.

## Daily transport

Cedar House, Nova Dining Hall, and Orion Building are within the pedestrian campus zone. The longest walk is approximately eight minutes. A step-free covered route connects all three buildings. Personal cars require a campus permit; this guide does not specify permit prices or public parking rates.

## Departure day: Saturday, 15 August

Departure shuttles leave **Cedar House main entrance** for Northstar Central Station at **08:30, 10:30, and 12:30** on Saturday, 15 August. Reserve a seat with the Residence Desk by 18:00 on 13 August. Residence checkout remains 10:00; participants taking the 12:30 shuttle must leave their luggage in the tagged storage area after returning their key.

## Delays and lost property

For a delay on arrival day, contact the Program Office. For an item left on a Northstar shuttle, contact Campus Services and provide the route time, seat area, and a description of the item. Emergency matters should follow the Safety Guide rather than the lost-property process.
