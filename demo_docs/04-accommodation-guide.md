# Northstar Summer Lab 2026 — Accommodation Guide

> Cedar House and all contact details in this document are fictional and exist only for the Tracefold demonstration.

## Eligibility and location

Program-provided accommodation is at **Cedar House**, inside Meridian Innovation Campus and about an eight-minute step-free walk from Orion Building. Only participants whose confirmation states “resident” have a room reservation. Rooms are single occupancy with a shared bathroom between two rooms.

## Check-in and checkout

Standard check-in is **14:00–20:00 on Sunday, 2 August 2026** at the Cedar House reception desk. Bring a photo ID and program confirmation. The desk cannot issue a room key to another participant.

If arrival will be after 20:00, call the Residence Duty Phone **before 19:30** to arrange collection from the night porter. The number is in the Contacts Directory. The Travel Guide contains the separate arrival-shuttle procedure; notifying the residence does not reserve or delay a shuttle.

Checkout is by **10:00 on Saturday, 15 August 2026**. Return the room key and borrowed linen bag to reception. Luggage may be stored in the tagged ground-floor room until 12:15 for participants using the final departure shuttle.

## Meals and kitchens

Breakfast is served in Cedar House from 07:15 to 08:30. Weekday lunch is served in Nova Dining Hall as described in the Participant Handbook. Dinner is served in Cedar House from 18:00 to 19:30, including weekends.

Each floor has a small kitchenette with a refrigerator, kettle, and microwave. Open-flame appliances, hot plates, and personal cooking devices are prohibited. Food must be labeled with the participant's name and checkout date.

Dietary information submitted in the participant profile is shared with the catering team. New or changed dietary requirements should be sent to Participant Support using the Contacts Directory; mentors cannot update catering records.

## Access, visitors, and quiet hours

Room keys open the resident's floor and common areas. Visitors may enter the ground-floor lounge from 17:00 to 21:00 after signing in and must remain with their host. Visitors are never permitted in bedrooms or floor kitchens.

Quiet hours are **22:00–07:00 every day**. During quiet hours, calls should use headphones and group work should move to the ground-floor study room. The Code of Conduct applies in the residence as well as the teaching spaces.

## Problems and accessibility

Report a maintenance problem to the Residence Desk during desk hours. Water leaks, loss of electrical power, inability to secure a room, or being locked out after hours should go to the Residence Duty Phone. For fire, smoke, injury, or another immediate threat, follow the Safety Guide first.

Accessible rooms and shower chairs are available by prior arrangement through Accessibility Support. The residence has lift access and an evacuation chair on each upper floor.
