# Northstar Summer Lab 2026 — Equipment and Technology Guide

> Product names are omitted intentionally. This is synthetic demo content for Tracefold.

## Participant laptop

Bring a laptop that can run a current 64-bit operating system and that meets these recommended minimums:

- **16 GB RAM**
- **60 GB free storage** after system updates
- Wi-Fi 5 or newer
- A working browser and USB-C or USB-A port
- Permission to install software and local development tools

Complete operating-system updates before arrival. Participants using an employer- or school-managed device should confirm they have local installation rights. A tablet alone is not sufficient for the project work.

## Loan laptops

A limited number of loan laptops are available to participants who requested one in their profile by **24 July 2026**. Collection is from the Equipment Desk in Orion Building between 08:00 and 08:45 on Monday, 3 August. Bring photo ID and sign the equipment receipt.

Loan laptops must remain on campus unless the Equipment Desk has recorded written permission. Return the laptop, charger, and sleeve by 17:30 on Friday, 14 August. Damage or loss should be reported immediately to the Equipment Desk; participants should not attempt hardware repair.

## Software and network

The setup clinic begins at 10:30 on Monday, 3 August. Installation packages and project datasets are distributed from the local campus file share. The teaching environment is designed to continue during an internet outage, although optional external links may be unavailable.

Do not disable endpoint protection, share access credentials, or connect an unapproved network bridge. Project teams may use removable storage supplied by the program, but personal USB drives must be scanned at the Equipment Desk first.

## Fabrication equipment

The program supplies safety glasses, antistatic mats, microcontroller kits, hand tools, inspected soldering stations, and consumable materials. Safety glasses must be worn during every Fabrication Studio session. Gloves are provided when a process requires them; they are not a substitute for machine guards.

Fabrication equipment is available only after the mandatory safety sign-off described in the Safety Guide. Tools may not leave the Fabrication Studio.

## Project data and end-of-program cleanup

Store working files in the assigned team workspace and keep at least one local backup. Do not place personal, medical, or confidential third-party data in project datasets. The team handoff is due at 17:00 on 14 August according to the Program Schedule; the 17:30 loan-device return deadline is **30 minutes later**.

At the end of the program, copy permitted project artifacts before returning a loan laptop. The Equipment Desk wipes loan devices after return. The program does not recover files after a device has been accepted and wiped.
