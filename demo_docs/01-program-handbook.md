# Northstar Summer Lab 2026 — Participant Handbook

> **Demo document:** Northstar Summer Lab is a fictional program created for the Tracefold local RAG demonstration. Names, addresses, and contact details are synthetic.

## Program at a glance

Northstar Summer Lab is a two-week, project-based technology program for university students. The 2026 session runs from **Monday, 3 August through Friday, 14 August 2026** at **Meridian Innovation Campus, Orion Building, 42 Polaris Avenue, Ankara**. The program language is English.

The standard weekday program runs from **09:00 to 17:00**. Participants should be in their assigned studio by 08:50. The opening briefing begins at 09:00 on 3 August in Aurora Auditorium. The final showcase begins at 14:00 on 14 August in the same auditorium; guests may enter from 13:30.

## Before arrival

Complete the participant profile and emergency-contact form by **24 July 2026**. Bring a government-issued photo ID for campus check-in. Participants under 18 must also bring the signed consent form supplied during registration.

Resident participants should follow the check-in and quiet-hour rules in the Accommodation Guide. Arrival transport, shuttle stops, and luggage rules are in the Travel Guide. Questions about an exception should go to the relevant contact listed in the Contacts Directory rather than to individual mentors.

## Daily rhythm

Each weekday normally includes a morning seminar, a coached project block, lunch, and an afternoon lab. Lunch is provided from **12:15 to 13:15** in the Nova Dining Hall. Participants must wear their badge while on campus and return reusable lunch tokens at the end of each meal.

Wednesday, 5 August includes a mandatory safety induction before any fabrication work. The detailed timetable, room assignments, showcase milestones, and optional evening events are maintained in the Program Schedule.

## Attendance

Attendance is required for the opening briefing, safety induction, team checkpoint, rehearsal, and final showcase. If illness or a travel disruption will make a participant more than 15 minutes late, they must notify the Program Office using the number in the Contacts Directory. A mentor cannot record an absence on the participant's behalf.

Participants who miss the safety induction may continue software-only work but cannot enter the Fabrication Studio until they complete a make-up induction with the Safety Officer.

## What to bring

Bring a laptop, charger, reusable water bottle, notebook, closed-toe shoes, and one government-issued photo ID. Laptop specifications, loan-device collection, supported software, and workshop protective equipment are described in the Equipment Guide.

Do not bring soldering irons, personal drones, laser devices, chemical kits, or other workshop tools. The lab supplies inspected equipment for supervised sessions.

## Help and wellbeing

The Program Office is the first point of contact for scheduling, attendance, accessibility coordination, and general help. For urgent safety matters, follow the emergency procedure in the Safety Guide. The Code of Conduct explains confidential reporting routes and the no-retaliation policy.
